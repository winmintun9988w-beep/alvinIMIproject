THINGS TO DO

Experiences at Marina Bay Sands

From skyline views to shopping and shows, there is always something new to discover.

DISCOVER MORE

More Than a Place to Stay

Marina Bay Sands is a destination for relaxing, exploring and having fun. Enjoy views from the rooftop, swim above the city, stay active in the gym, shop, watch a show or explore art and culture.

FEATURED EXPERIENCE

Sands SkyPark

A rooftop viewing area high above the bay, with wide views of the city skyline, the sea and the gardens.

  • Best for: Everyone, especially photo lovers
  • Access: Hours and tickets vary (sample)
Sunset is a popular time to visit. Check the official website for current opening hours and ticket prices.
01

Infinity Pool

Swim in a rooftop pool that seems to meet the sky, with the city spread out below.

  • Best for: Hotel guests and couples
  • Access: Usually for hotel guests only (sample)
Pool hours and rules can change, so check at the hotel reception before you go.
02

Fitness Centre

Keep up your routine with modern equipment and a calm, bright space to train.

  • Best for: Guests who like to stay active
  • Access: Open most of the day (sample)
Includes cardio machines and weights. Bring sports shoes and a towel is provided (sample information).
03

Shopping

Browse a wide mix of fashion, beauty, gifts and local products under one roof.

  • Best for: Families, friends and gift hunters
  • Access: Open to the public daily (sample)
Many shops are indoors, so it is a good choice on a rainy day. Opening hours differ by shop.
04

Entertainment

Enjoy live shows, theatre and evening events in a lively setting.

  • Best for: Families and groups of friends
  • Access: Tickets for each show (sample)
Show times and ticket prices change often, so please check the latest schedule before planning your evening.
05

Art & Culture

Explore exhibitions and creative displays that show off art, design and ideas.

  • Best for: Students, families and art lovers
  • Access: Ticketed exhibitions (sample)
Exhibitions change through the year. Look at the official website to see what is on during your visit.

PLAN AHEAD

Good to Know

Opening Hours

Times are different for each attraction, so check before you visit.

Tickets

Some experiences are free and some need a ticket or hotel booking.

Best Time

February to April: Best weather and outdoor sightseeing around Marina Bay.
