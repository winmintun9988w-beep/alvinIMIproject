ABOUT MARINA BAY SANDS

An Icon of Singapore

Marina Bay Sands is an integrated resort located along the Marina Bay waterfront in Singapore.

Since opening in 2010, Marina Bay Sands has become one of Singapore's most recognisable landmarks.

The destination features three hotel towers connected by the spectacular Sands SkyPark, together with dining, shopping, entertainment and cultural experiences.

The hotel offers approximately 1,850 rooms and suites across its three towers.

Guests can also enjoy access to the famous SkyPark Infinity Pool and other exclusive hotel experiences.
