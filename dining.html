OUR RESTAURANTS

Find Your Table

Skyline Grill

Western • Fine Dining

Premium steaks and fresh seafood served with a view of the city skyline.

Location: Tower 2, Level 55 (sample)

Hours: 6:00 pm - 11:00 pm (sample)

A smart-casual restaurant, ideal for anniversaries and special dinners. Reservations are recommended.

Sakura Room

Japanese • Fine Dining

A calm, elegant restaurant serving sushi, sashimi and seasonal set menus.

Location: Tower 1, Level 2 (sample)

Hours: 6:30 pm - 10:30 pm (sample)

Guests can choose a multi-course tasting menu or order individual dishes. Best for couples and small groups.

Hawker Lane

Asian • Casual Dining

A food court style hall with local favourites such as noodles, rice dishes and satay.

Location: Shopping level, B2 (sample)

Hours: 10:00 am - 10:00 pm (sample)

A relaxed place with many stalls to choose from. Great for families and guests on a budget. No reservation needed.

Poolside Cafe

Western • Casual Dining

Burgers, salads, sandwiches and cold drinks to enjoy after a swim.

Location: Tower 2, Pool Deck (sample)

Hours: 11:00 am - 8:00 pm (sample)

A laid-back cafe with outdoor seating. Light meals and snacks are served all day.
